What We Do

Better coordination. Fewer avoidable delays.

Large furnishing projects can involve many factories, product specifications, samples, inspections and shipments. ZORAYA GROUP helps bring those moving parts together through a clear and coordinated supply process.

We focus on furniture sourcing and supply, manufacturing follow-up, quality checks and logistics coordination. Specialist interior design and fit-out services can be arranged separately through our independent partner network when a project requires them.

Furniture Sourcing

Identify suitable suppliers and product options against the approved brief.

Custom Manufacturing

Coordinate made-to-order furniture, finishes, samples and specifications.

Quality Inspection

Check products against agreed samples, drawings and project requirements.

Quotation Management

Clear itemised quotations with quantities, pricing and FOB terms where applicable.

Container Planning

Consolidate orders and coordinate loading plans to use shipment space efficiently.

Export & Logistics

Coordinate with suppliers and freight partners for export and delivery planning.

Installation Coordination

Work with local installation teams and project representatives where required.

Project Communication

Keep the client, suppliers and delivery partners aligned throughout the process.

Our Process

From design to door-to-door delivery.

One coordinated project journey, bringing together specialist design and installation, furniture supply, quality checks and delivery to your project destination.

01

Design & Plan

Where required, ZORAYA GROUP can coordinate with independent specialist providers for interior design and fit-out services under a separately agreed project scope.

02

Source & Manufacture

ZORAYA coordinates suitable suppliers, custom furniture production, materials, finishes, samples and quotations.

03

Quality Control

We follow production and check goods against approved samples, specifications and agreed quality requirements before dispatch.

04

Door-to-Door Delivery

We can arrange export, freight, customs coordination and delivery to the project destination, subject to the agreed scope.

05

Installation & Handover

ZORAYA GROUP can coordinate furniture delivery and work with independently appointed local teams for installation or fit-out where included in the agreed project scope.
