3D Visualisation & Presentation Documents

See the proposed result before you proceed.

Where requested, ZORAYA GROUP can coordinate 3D concepts and visual presentation documents through independent specialist providers, so clients can review proposed furniture, finishes and the overall direction before procurement or production.

Project presentation pack

Presentation format and level of detail are agreed for each project. Packs can be prepared in PowerPoint or PDF and may include:

  • 3D layouts for reception, work areas and meeting rooms
  • Furniture plans and workstation configurations
  • Fabric, leather, colour and finish boards
  • Product specifications, dimensions and quantities
  • Ergonomic and functional review points for selected furniture
  • PowerPoint or PDF presentation documents for approval

Material & finish review

Clients can compare the look and feel of proposed materials through coordinated samples, finish boards and specification details. This can include fabric texture, leather feel, colour, wood grain, marble pattern, stainless steel or decorative metal finishes, wallpaper and wall-covering options.

Final selections, product dimensions, specifications, quantities and approved samples are recorded for supplier coordination and quality checks.

Project Coordination

One coordinated process, with clear responsibilities.

We coordinate furniture sourcing, custom manufacturing options, supplier communication, samples, quality checks and delivery planning. Furniture specifications can be reviewed with the client's appointed workplace designer, facilities team or project consultant. Specialist fit-out and building services remain within the scope of separately appointed providers. ZORAYA GROUP acts as the client's furniture sourcing and project coordination partner. Specialist design, fit-out, installation and other site services are delivered by separately appointed or selected independent providers under clearly agreed project scopes; responsibilities and commercial arrangements are confirmed for each project.

From First Brief to Final Handover

A complete furnishing journey.

Delivery can be coordinated around office moves, floor-by-floor installation or phased occupancy. Where included in the agreed scope, we can coordinate furniture installation support, professional post-installation deep cleaning and a final walkthrough with the client's project or facilities team.
