What We Do

Furniture supply with project coordination.

From product sourcing to export coordination, ZORAYA helps connect the stages of a furniture supply package. Each service is defined around the confirmed project requirements and agreed responsibilities.

01

Furniture sourcing & supply

Source loose furniture, casegoods, seating, bedroom and dining furniture, and project-specific product packages.

02

Custom furniture

Coordinate made-to-order furniture requirements, finishes, dimensions, samples and approved specifications.

03

Manufacturing coordination

Coordinate enquiries, quotations, production schedules and communication with selected manufacturers.

04

Quality inspection

Arrange agreed quality checks against approved drawings, samples and project specifications.

05

Export & container coordination

Coordinate packing information, shipment planning, container loading and export arrangements according to agreed trade terms.

06

Project delivery & installation arrangements

Coordinate delivery planning and, where required, arrange installation support through independently appointed local teams.

Specialist delivery

Clear scopes. The right specialists.

Interior design, fit-out and installation may be undertaken by independent specialist companies engaged for specific project scopes. For example, MD Interior Design & Trading is an independent service provider; it is not owned by or part of ZORAYA Group. Contracting parties and responsibilities are confirmed project by project.
