What We Do

Furniture supply with project coordination.

From furniture sourcing and custom manufacturing to 3D visualisation, quality inspection, export and container coordination, site coordination, installation arrangements and door-to-door delivery, ZORAYA coordinates the agreed project supply scope.

01

Furniture sourcing & supply

Source loose furniture, casegoods, seating, bedroom and dining furniture, and project-specific product packages.

02

Custom furniture

Coordinate made-to-order furniture requirements, finishes, dimensions, samples and approved specifications.

03

Manufacturing coordination

Coordinate enquiries, quotations, production schedules and communication with selected manufacturers.

04

Quality inspection

Arrange agreed quality checks against approved drawings, samples and project specifications.

05

Export & container coordination

Coordinate packing information, shipment planning, container loading and export arrangements according to agreed trade terms.

06

3D design & visualisation

Where requested, coordinate 3D concepts and visual presentations through independent specialist providers to review layouts, furniture scale, materials and finishes before production.

07

Project coordination

Keep the client, manufacturers, logistics providers and appointed specialists aligned around the agreed scope, approvals, programme and delivery milestones.

08

Professional installation

Arrange delivery scheduling, site access and installation coordination with the client’s appointed project team. Depending on the agreed package, this may include furniture assembly, placement and fixing by independently appointed local providers.

09

Door-to-door delivery

Coordinate collection from manufacturers, export packing, shipment planning and transport to the nominated project address. Customs and destination handling can be coordinated with appointed logistics partners where included in the written scope.

10

Site coordination & handover

Coordinate delivery sequences, room or area allocations, site communication and agreed final checks. Record outstanding items and arrange follow-up on verified non-conformities within the confirmed supply scope.

Design Detail & Product Selection

See the details before production begins.

For projects that require it, ZORAYA can coordinate presentation material and 3D visualisations through independent specialist providers. These help the client review the proposed look, room layout, furniture proportions and finishes before specifications are approved and manufacturing is instructed.

01 / VISUALISATION

3D room concepts & presentations

Room views, furniture placement and coordinated design presentations can help stakeholders understand how the proposed spaces may look. Final drawings, dimensions and technical details remain subject to review and approval.

02 / COMFORT & DIMENSIONS

Size, fit & feel

Review seating depth, seat height, mattress and bed dimensions, table heights, circulation space and practical use. Where required, samples or prototypes can be reviewed so comfort and proportions are considered—not just appearance.

03 / MATERIALS & FINISHES

Choose the right materials

Coordinate options for upholstery fabrics, leather, timber and veneer, metal finishes including stainless steel, stone and other surfaces, wallpaper, wall panels, curtains and decorative details.

Samples, specifications & approvals

Material swatches, finish samples, hardware details, colour references and product specifications can be assembled for review. The approved selections are recorded as the reference for supplier coordination and agreed quality checks.

Quality control before dispatch

Inspection arrangements can check workmanship, dimensions, materials, finish consistency, packaging and quantities against the approved requirements. Any findings are recorded and discussed for resolution before shipment, within the agreed inspection scope.

From Factory to Final Handover

One coordinated journey. From first brief to a clean, ready space.

ZORAYA GROUP can coordinate the agreed furniture supply journey across selected manufacturers, logistics providers and independent on-site specialists. The exact responsibilities, providers and deliverables are confirmed in writing for each project.

01 · Plan & approve

Understand the project brief, room schedules, quantities, budget, dimensions, materials, samples and target programme.

02 · Manufacture & inspect

Coordinate production progress and agreed quality inspections, then address recorded issues before dispatch where possible.

03 · Ship & deliver

Coordinate export packing, container planning, freight and delivery arrangements to the nominated destination.

04 · Install & place

Coordinate site access, delivery sequence, furniture assembly, placement and fixing with the appointed installation team.

05 · Clean & prepare

Where included in the agreed scope, arrange a post-installation deep clean with an independent cleaning provider—removing installation dust and packaging residue and cleaning the furnished areas for handover.

06 · Final checks & handover

Coordinate a final walk-through, note outstanding items and arrange agreed follow-up so the client can review the completed spaces.

Installation, specialist trades, deep cleaning, customs, freight and other site services are provided or arranged only where expressly included in the written project scope. Specialist providers remain independent, and their responsibilities are confirmed project by project.
