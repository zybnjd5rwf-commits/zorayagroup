About ZORAYA

Project supply, thoughtfully coordinated.

ZORAYA Group is an international furniture supply and project solutions company supporting hospitality, residential and commercial developments. We help clients source, coordinate and deliver furniture packages with clear communication from the initial brief through production and shipping.

Our role

Your project supply partner.

We bring together suitable manufacturers and specialist suppliers around each project's scope, design intent, specification, budget and programme. Our role is to coordinate the supply process and keep key details visible—so clients can make informed decisions and reduce avoidable delays.

How we work
  • Understand the project brief and scope
  • Source products and suitable manufacturing partners
  • Coordinate samples, specifications and quotations
  • Follow production and agreed quality checks
  • Coordinate packing, export and delivery arrangements
Our promise

Clarity at every stage.

Project responsibilities, specifications, quality criteria, delivery terms and installation arrangements are agreed for each individual project. Specialist design, fit-out and installation services may be delivered by independent partners under separately agreed scopes.
