03 · Comfort, Safety & Performance

A better hotel is more than a beautiful photograph.

Guests also expect privacy, quiet, reliable facilities and a safe environment. The right technical specification matters as much as the visual finish.

Acoustic Improvements

Acoustic wall panels, suitable insulation, door seals and sound-reducing assemblies can be considered to help reduce unwanted noise, subject to assessment and specification.

Fire-Rated Doors

60-minute fire-rated door assemblies may be required in specified locations. Ratings, hardware, installation and certification must follow the approved fire strategy and authority requirements.

Bathrooms & Finishes

Updated fittings, vanity furniture, durable surfaces and coordinated finishes can help create a cleaner, more contemporary guest experience.

For Dubai projects, fire and life-safety requirements must be checked against the applicable codes, approved drawings, building conditions and authority approvals by the appointed qualified consultants. A 60-minute rating is not automatically required for every door.
