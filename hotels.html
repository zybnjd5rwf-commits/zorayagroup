3D Visualisation & Presentation Documents

See the proposed result before you proceed.

Where requested, ZORAYA GROUP can coordinate 3D concepts and visual presentation documents through independent specialist providers, so clients can review proposed furniture, finishes and the overall direction before procurement or production.

Project presentation pack

Presentation format and level of detail are agreed for each project. Packs can be prepared in PowerPoint or PDF and may include:

  • Room-by-room 3D visualisations and furniture placement
  • Guest-room and public-area furniture schedules
  • Fabric, leather, colour and finish boards
  • Product dimensions, specifications and quantities
  • PowerPoint or PDF presentation packs, as agreed
  • Sample review and approval records

Material & finish review

Clients can compare the look and feel of proposed materials through coordinated samples, finish boards and specification details. This can include fabric texture, leather feel, colour, wood grain, marble pattern, stainless steel or decorative metal finishes, wallpaper and wall-covering options.

Final selections, product dimensions, specifications, quantities and approved samples are recorded for supplier coordination and quality checks.

Project Coordination

One coordinated process, with clear responsibilities.

We help owners, operators, procurement teams and appointed consultants define the furniture schedule, compare suppliers, coordinate custom production, review samples and check goods against approved specifications. Installation and specialist fit-out can be coordinated with independent providers where included in the agreed project scope. ZORAYA GROUP acts as the client's furniture sourcing and project coordination partner. Specialist design, fit-out, installation and other site services are delivered by separately appointed or selected independent providers under clearly agreed project scopes; responsibilities and commercial arrangements are confirmed for each project.
